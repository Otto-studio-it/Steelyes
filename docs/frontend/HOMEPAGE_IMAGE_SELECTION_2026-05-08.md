## Rejected For Homepage

- Product finial cut-outs on white background: useful for catalogue/admin later, but too product-isolated for the homepage.
- Van, business-card, shirt and branding images: not suitable for public homepage product proof.
- Balcony, stair, glass and railing jobs: useful for service pages, but they dilute the homepage gate focus.
- Images with people clearly visible or obvious private identifiers: avoid until publishing consent is confirmed.
- Low-resolution or awkward vertical crops: keep for gallery only if needed.

## Privacy And Approval Notes

These are real client/project photos. Before staging is shared externally, confirm permission to publish residential property images. Avoid showing visible number plates, faces, house numbers or private signage unless cleared.

The selected hero and card images were chosen because they show the actual product clearly and have no obvious faces or readable number plates in the selected crop.
