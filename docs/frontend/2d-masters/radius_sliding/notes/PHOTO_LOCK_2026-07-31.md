## Decisions

3. Travel path always curved (CA-12); park ~90° beside wall shown as plan cue.
4. Panel count schematic: 4 / 5 (≥2200) / 6 (≥3000) — photo language beats coarse intake 2/3.
5. `arched_top` → curved crest on **every** panel.
