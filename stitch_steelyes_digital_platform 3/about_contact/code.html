Based in the heart of the industrial North, Steelyes Ltd combines centuries-old blacksmithing techniques with contemporary CAD precision. We don't just manufacture gates; we engineer structural statements.

The Workshop Ethos

Our workshop is where raw iron meets digital accuracy. Every project begins with a 3D structural analysis before the first cut is even made. This marriage of high-tech planning and manual execution ensures that every gate we produce is balanced to within a millimeter.

We source our steel exclusively from British foundries, ensuring a carbon footprint that respects the landscape our products eventually guard.
