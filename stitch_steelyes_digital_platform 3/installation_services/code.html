The Installation Blueprint

Precision at every milestone. Our process is designed for minimal disruption and maximum structural longevity.

01

Technical Survey

02

03

04

Clarification

Do you handle electrical connections for automation?

expand_more

Yes. Our installation teams include Part P certified electricians who manage everything from the main power feed to the integration of wireless intercoms and access control systems.

How long does a typical driveway install take?

expand_more

A standard automated swing gate installation takes between 3 to 5 working days, which includes ground preparation, curing of concrete foundations, and mechanical rigging.

Are your installers Gate Safe certified?

expand_more

Every Steelyes installer is Gate Safe certified. We conduct a full force-test assessment and safety compliance audit on every automated system we commission.

Can you install onto existing stone pillars?

expand_more

We provide a structural analysis during the survey. If required, we can reinforce existing pillars with internal steel sleeve systems to handle the dynamic load of automated gates.
